## 周二-2230-轻互动帖-黑独山你让我怎么跟我朋友圈交代

Black Lone Mountain, the one place on the Qinghai-Gansu loop that broke my feed.

Bus loads of people on every photogenic ridge, a queue for the one good angle, drones overhead. The detour is a 6 km walk on gravel.

Go at 7am or don't go. And bring patience.

中文：黑独山实测：人比山多，想拍空镜只能赶早。

## 周五-2100-Flagship-Thread-贵州｜一次性讲清楚贵州游玩路线别再乱走

Guizhou in one route, no zig-zagging: Guiyang → Anshun → Libo → Kaili → Zhenyuan → Tongren.

## 周六-0800-四图宫格单图帖-迄今为止…我最喜欢的旅游小镇出现了！

Yixing's kiln town, seen properly this time: birds at dusk, a fire-and-water show at 8, pottery streets, and a valley that looks like a Monet painting in May.

Two days. Sleep at the kiln hotel. Bring a light jacket for the night show.

中文：宜兴窑湖小镇：白天看鸟、晚上看水秀烟花。

## 周六-2100-视频切片-318（一）｜5分钟了解318路况

318 in five minutes, road conditions only: Zheduo Pass snow, the 72 turns, truck traffic between Batang and Basu, and the long empty stretch after Bomi.

Plan 300 km a day, not 500. The road decides your schedule, not you.

中文：318路况5分钟：折多山雪、72拐、巴塘到八宿大车多。
