# X 短帖

## EN (<=280)

At 20, ¥10,000 bought me half of China: night trains, a sunrise I climbed for, a wrong turn into a wheat field.

At 60, ¥10,000 buys one night in a hospital bed and one more sunset you can't walk to.

Spend it early. The view doesn't wait.

二十岁的1万块买体验，六十岁的1万块买时间。早点花。
