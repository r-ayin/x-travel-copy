# X 短帖

## EN (<=280)

The whole ring, three roads: G331 north (9,300 km), G219 west (10,065 km), G228 coast (7,800 km). Start and finish at Dongxing.

80-120 days. Four extremes, fourteen borders. May to October if you want to avoid snow.
